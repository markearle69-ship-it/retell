How this works

Self-hosted, cookie-free page-view tracking — no Google Analytics, no Search Console, no shared script or account across your sites. Add a site below, then paste its snippet just before </body> on that microsite. No cookies or localStorage are used, and no raw IP address or full user-agent string is ever stored — see the README for details.

On search queries: Google strips the search term from its referrer for organic results (this is why every analytics tool shows "(not provided)" for Google organic) — that's a Google limitation, not something this tool can work around. Bing, DuckDuckGo, Yahoo, Yandex and Baidu still pass the term through and are captured below. For guaranteed keyword-level data (including from Google), set utm_term={keyword} as a Google Ads ValueTrack parameter (or the Bing Ads equivalent) on your ad destination URLs.
